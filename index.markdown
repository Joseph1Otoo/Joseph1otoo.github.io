## Speaking through data for the common good

I am an **AI researcher** at the **Centre for Research, Data Science and IT
Solutions**, working on mathematical and statistical modelling, machine
learning, and deep learning.

I started in optimization, moved into statistical and mathematical modelling,
and now work on AI explainability, trustworthy research, and climate. That arc
runs from irrigation allocation and crop selection on Ghanaian smallholder
farms, through epidemic forecasting, malaria treatment uptake and monetary
policy, to activation functions for disease classification and natural language
processing for Ghanaian languages.

The applied problems change. The question does not: what can honestly be
inferred from the data available, and does anyone expected to act on the answer
have reason to trust it?

- **[Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }})** — peer-reviewed

- **[Research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }})** — notes on the methods and
