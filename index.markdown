## Speaking through data for the common good

I am an AI researcher at the **Centre for Research, Data Science and IT
Solutions**, working on explainable AI, statistical modelling, and climate and
health. The question underneath all of it: what can honestly be inferred from
the data available, and does anyone expected to act on the answer have reason
to trust it?
