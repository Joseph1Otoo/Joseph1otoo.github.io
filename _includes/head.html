{%- comment -%} Overrides minima 2.5's _includes/head.html. The only change is the removal of minima's feed-meta tag (written with Liquid braces in the original; spelled out here so nothing tries to parse it). That tag emits the RSS autodiscovery , which we do not want. Note the tag still WORKS — jekyll-feed is a gemspec runtime dependency of minima, and Jekyll auto-requires a theme's runtime deps, so the plugin loads even though it appears in neither `plugins:` nor the Gemfile. This override is about suppressing the link, not about avoiding an undefined tag. To bring RSS back: delete this file, drop the `rm -f _site/feed.xml` line from the build workflow, and give index.markdown `layout: home` again. {%- endcomment -%} {%- seo -%} {%- if jekyll.environment == 'production' and site.google_analytics -%} {%- include google-analytics.html -%} {%- endif -%}
